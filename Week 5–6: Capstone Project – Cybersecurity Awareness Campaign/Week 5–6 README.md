# Week 5–6: Capstone Project – Cybersecurity Awareness Campaign
# ALGURAWY CYBERHUB ACADEMY  
# Sponsored by DR. BUKAR USMAN FOUNDATION & IRIAD   

---

## 📘 Overview
The final two weeks are dedicated to preparing and presenting a **Cybersecurity Awareness Campaign**. Students will work in groups of 10 to design and deliver practical awareness materials that address real-life cybersecurity challenges faced by staff and students.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Raise awareness of common cyber threats (phishing, weak passwords, unsafe browsing).  
- Create engaging training materials (posters, slides, short videos, role-plays).  
- Deliver a live presentation to demonstrate knowledge and communication skills.  
- Showcase teamwork, creativity, and technical understanding.  

---

## 📑 Project Deliverables
Each group must produce:
1. **Awareness Toolkit**  
   - Posters or infographics (digital or printed).  
   - Short video or skit (2–3 minutes).  
   - Presentation slides (PowerPoint/Google Slides).  

2. **Live Presentation (10–15 minutes)**  
   - Explain the chosen cybersecurity topic.  
   - Demonstrate the awareness materials.  
   - Engage the audience with practical examples.  

3. **Written Report (5–7 pages)**  
   - Introduction to the chosen topic.  
   - Explanation of risks and real-life relevance.  
   - Description of awareness materials created.  
   - Reflection on teamwork and lessons learned.  

---

## 📘 Suggested Topics
Groups may choose one of the following focus areas:
- **[Phishing Awareness](ca://s?q=Capstone_Project_on_Phishing_Awareness)** → How to spot fake emails and links.  
- **[Password Hygiene](ca://s?q=Capstone_Project_on_Password_Hygiene)** → Importance of strong, unique passwords.  
- **[Safe Browsing](ca://s?q=Capstone_Project_on_Safe_Browsing)** → Avoiding malicious websites and downloads.  
- **[Social Engineering](ca://s?q=Capstone_Project_on_Social_Engineering)** → Recognizing manipulation tactics.  
- **[Mobile Security](ca://s?q=Capstone_Project_on_Mobile_Security)** → Protecting smartphones from malware and theft.  

---

## 📘 Group Roles
Each group of 5 should assign roles:
- **Content Researcher** → Gathers facts and examples.  
- **Designer** → Creates posters/infographics.  
- **Video/Skits Producer** → Directs short video or role-play.  
- **Presenter** → Leads the live presentation.  
- **Reporter** → Compiles the written report.  

---

## 📜 Grading Criteria
- **Content Accuracy (30%)** → Correct, relevant cybersecurity information.  
- **Creativity (20%)** → Engaging posters, videos, and presentations.  
- **Presentation Skills (20%)** → Clear, confident delivery to audience.  
- **Teamwork (15%)** → Balanced contribution from all members.  
- **Report Quality (15%)** → Well-structured, professional documentation.  

---

## 🧪 Timeline
- **Week 5** → Research, design materials, draft report.  
- **Week 6** → Finalize toolkit, rehearse, and present at closing ceremony.  

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
This capstone project ensures students not only understand cybersecurity but can **communicate it effectively** to protect their community. It balances technical knowledge with real-world impact, making it ideal for presentation to senior officers.
