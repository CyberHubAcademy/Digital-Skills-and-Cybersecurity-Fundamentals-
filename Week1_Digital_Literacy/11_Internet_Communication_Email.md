## 🎯 Learning Objectives
By the end of this session, students should be able to:
- Understand the basics of internet communication.
- Use email effectively for professional correspondence.
- Apply proper etiquette in online communication.
- Manage attachments and links securely.
- Recognize cybersecurity risks in email usage.

---

## 🌐 Internet Communication Basics
- **Email**: Sending and receiving digital messages.  
- **Instant Messaging**: Real-time text communication (e.g., WhatsApp, Teams, Slack).  
- **Video Conferencing**: Online meetings via Zoom, Google Meet, Teams.  
- **Social Media**: Platforms for networking and sharing information.  
- **Forums & Blogs**: Online communities for discussion and publishing.  

---

## 📧 Email Components
- **To**: Primary recipient(s).  
- **Cc (Carbon Copy)**: Secondary recipients who should be informed.  
- **Bcc (Blind Carbon Copy)**: Hidden recipients.  
- **Subject Line**: Brief summary of the email’s purpose.  
- **Body**: Main message content.  
- **Attachments**: Files sent with the email.  
- **Signature**: Contact details and professional sign‑off.  

---

## 🖋️ Writing Professional Emails
- **Greeting**: Start with “Dear [Name]” or “Hello [Name]”.  
- **Introduction**: State purpose clearly.  
- **Details**: Provide necessary information concisely.  
- **Closing**: End with “Regards,” “Sincerely,” or “Thank you.”  
- **Signature**: Include name, role, and contact info.  

---

## 📑 Email Etiquette
- Use clear subject lines.  
- Keep messages concise and polite.  
- Avoid slang or informal language in professional emails.  
- Reply promptly to important messages.  
- Do not use ALL CAPS (it looks like shouting).  
- Proofread before sending.  

---

## 🔒 Cybersecurity in Email
- **Attachments**: Open only trusted files.  
- **Links**: Verify URLs before clicking.  
- **Phishing**: Be cautious of suspicious requests for personal info.  
- **Passwords**: Never share via email.  
- **Spam**: Avoid responding to unknown senders.
