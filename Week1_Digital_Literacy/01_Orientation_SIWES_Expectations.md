## 🎯 Objectives
By the end of this session, students should be able to:
- **Explain the purpose of SIWES**: Understand why the Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme exists — to bridge classroom theory with workplace practice.  
- **Describe participant expectations**: Know what behaviours and responsibilities are required during training.  
- **Identify career opportunities in the digital economy**: Recognize fields like cybersecurity, software development, cloud computing, and data analysis.  
- **Demonstrate responsible behaviour**: Show professionalism, punctuality, and ethical conduct in computing environments.  

---

## 📘 SIWES Principles
- **Learning about technology vs. learning to use technology**:  
  - *About*: Knowing definitions and theory (e.g., “An operating system manages hardware”).  
  - *Use*: Practical competence (e.g., installing Windows or Ubuntu, troubleshooting errors).  
- **Transforming theory into practice**: The programme’s goal is to ensure students can demonstrate skills, not just recite definitions.  

---

## 📋 Expectations of Participants
- **Attend sessions regularly**: Consistency builds discipline and ensures you don’t miss practicals.  
- **Arrive on time**: Punctuality reflects professionalism.  
- **Maintain professionalism**: Respect trainers, colleagues, and lab rules.  
- **Document practical activities**: Keep a SIWES logbook and record what you did each day.  
- **Protect equipment**: Handle computers and devices carefully.  
- **Follow cybersecurity and ethical rules**: Don’t misuse systems or exploit vulnerabilities.  
- **Work cooperatively**: Teamwork is essential in real workplaces.  
- **Ask questions**: Clarify doubts early to avoid mistakes.

## 🌐 Career Opportunities in the Digital Economy
The digital economy is powered by computing, data, and networks. Career paths include:
- **Cybersecurity**: Protecting systems and data.  
- **Software Development**: Building applications and solutions.  
- **Networking**: Designing and maintaining computer networks.  
- **Cloud Computing**: Managing scalable online services.  
- **Database Administration**: Organizing and securing data.  
- **Data Analysis**: Extracting insights from information.  
- **Digital Forensics**: Investigating cyber incidents.  
- **Web Development**: Creating websites and online platforms.  
- **Digital Marketing**: Promoting products and services online.
