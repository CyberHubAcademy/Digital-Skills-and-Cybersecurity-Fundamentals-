## 📘 What is Google Workspace?
Google Workspace (formerly G Suite) is a **cloud-based productivity suite** that includes tools for creating, storing, and sharing documents online.  
It allows multiple users to collaborate in real time from any device with internet access.

---

## 🖼️ Key Applications
- **Google Docs**: Word processing.  
- **Google Sheets**: Spreadsheets.  
- **Google Slides**: Presentations.  
- **Google Drive**: Cloud storage.  
- **Gmail**: Email communication.  
- **Google Meet**: Video conferencing.  

---

## ✍️ Getting Started
1. Open a browser and go to [https://workspace.google.com](https://workspace.google.com).  
2. Sign in with your Gmail account.  
3. Access apps via the **Google Apps menu** (9-dot grid in the top-right corner).  

---

## 📑 Sharing and Privileges
When you share a file in Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides, you can assign different roles:

- **Viewer**:  
  - Can only read the document.  
  - Cannot make changes or add comments.  
  - Best for distributing final versions.  

- **Commenter**:  
  - Can read and add comments/suggestions.  
  - Cannot directly edit the document.  
  - Useful for peer review and feedback.  

- **Editor**:  
  - Full access to edit, format, and delete content.  
  - Can share with others and change permissions.  
  - Best for group projects requiring collaboration.  

⚠️ **Important**: Always assign the minimum privilege necessary. For example, don’t give “Editor” rights if someone only needs to review.  

---

## 📂 Google Drive
- Upload files: Click New → File Upload.  
- Organize files into folders.  
- Share files with peers using links or email addresses.  
- Control access with Viewer, Commenter, or Editor roles.  

---

## 📧 Gmail
- Compose new email: Click **Compose**.  
- Add recipients, subject, and message.  
- Attach files from computer or Google Drive.  
- Organize emails with labels and filters.  

---

## 🎥 Google Meet
- Start meeting: Google Apps → Meet → New Meeting.  
- Share meeting link with participants.  
- Features: Screen sharing, chat, recording (if enabled).
